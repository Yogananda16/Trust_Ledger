# Trust_Ledger

AI agent for startups and small businesses that detects suspicious vendors and payments before the money is sent. It verifies vendors using transaction history, scam patterns, and live web data, then explains exactly why a payment may be risky.

So you know how startups pay a ton of vendors — SaaS tools, contractors, suppliers — companies they've never actually met? Turns out that's a huge fraud target. Someone impersonates a vendor, sends a fake invoice or 'updated bank details,' and the company just pays it because nobody's actually checking who they're sending money to. It's a multi-billion-dollar problem every year, and small startups get hit way harder than big companies because they don't have a whole finance team watching for it.
